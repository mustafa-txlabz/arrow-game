Support & Data Deletion

Need help with puzzle controls, hints, revives, or deleting your saved progress? Find quick answers below or reach out to our team.

1. Frequently Asked Questions

How do hints and free hint ads work?

Tap the lightbulb button during any level to highlight an arrow with an open escape path. When your hint count reaches zero, the button displays an AD badge—tapping it lets you watch a short rewarded ad to earn +1 Hint. You can also earn hints from the Daily Bonus or the Home screen hint chip.

Can I continue if I lose all 3 hearts?

Yes. When all 3 hearts are lost, tap Watch Ad · Revive (3 Hearts) on the Out of Lives menu to restore all 3 hearts and resume the puzzle from the exact remaining arrows.

How do I zoom and pan large puzzle boards?

Pinch anywhere on the board with two fingers to zoom cleanly in vector resolution, drag with one finger to pan around dense grids, and tap the floating 1.0× button at the bottom-left to snap back to full view.

2. How to Delete Your Saved Data

Arrow Escape stores all level progress, hints, daily streaks, and audio preferences locally on your device rather than on external servers. You can delete all data immediately using either method:

  1. Inside the App: Open Settings (gear icon) → tap Reset Saved Progress → tap Confirm Reset.
  2. Android System Settings: Go to Device Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

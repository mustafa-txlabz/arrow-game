Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape handles player data, local device storage, permissions, and advertising across Android, iOS, and Web builds.

At a Glance

No Account Required Play immediately with zero sign-up, login, or profile forms.
On-Device Progress Levels, hints, daily streaks, and sound settings stay on your device.
No Sensitive Permissions No camera, microphone, contacts, or GPS location access.

1. Information We Collect & Local Storage

Arrow Escape is designed as an offline-first puzzle game. We do not operate user account servers and we do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

To preserve your game state between sessions, the app stores the following gameplay data strictly locally on your device (via AsyncStorage on mobile or browser localStorage on web):

  • Puzzle progression (currentLevel and highestUnlockedLevel)
  • Virtual hint balance (hints)
  • Daily reward tracking (lastDailyClaimDate and dailyStreak)
  • Audio and tactile preferences (sfx, music, and haptics)

Because puzzle boards are generated deterministically on your device using level seeds, no board history or gameplay telemetry is transmitted to our servers.

2. Device Permissions

Arrow Escape requests only the minimal device capabilities needed for gameplay feedback:

  • Audio & Vibration / Haptics: Used solely to play synthesized sound effects, optional ambient chords, and tactile tap feedback. Microphone recording is explicitly disabled (recordAudioAndroid: false).
  • Network Access (When Production Ads Are Active): Required only to fetch or display advertisements once live ad mediation is enabled.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Arrow Escape offers optional Rewarded Ad placements (earning +1 Hint when hints reach zero, claiming a free hint from the Home screen or Victory screen, and reviving with 3 hearts when out of lives) as well as periodic level-transition breaks.

  • Preview / Simulated Ad Mode: Builds running in preview mode display an internal, self-contained ad simulation screen and do not contact external ad servers or share device identifiers.
  • Production Ad Networks (e.g., Google AdMob): When live ads are enabled in Google Play Store releases, third-party ad partners such as Google AdMob may automatically process limited technical information—including your device's Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA), coarse IP-based region, device model, OS version, and ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, and reward completion)—to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure frequency caps.

Where required by regional law (such as EEA/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), consent choices or non-personalized ad settings will apply before personalized ad identifiers are used. You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device under Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). Because no registration or personal profile data is requested inside the app, no personal details are stored by us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal information was inadvertently submitted via support correspondence, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Controls

All gameplay progress and preferences remain on your device only for as long as you keep the app installed. You have full control to erase your data at any time:

  • In-App Wipe: Open Settings → Reset Saved Progress → Confirm Reset to immediately clear levels, hints, and daily streak data back to defaults.
  • Device Removal: Uninstalling Arrow Escape or clearing the app's storage in Android System Settings removes all locally stored records.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy whenever new features or third-party SDKs (such as production ad networks or cloud backups) are introduced. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised Effective Date.
